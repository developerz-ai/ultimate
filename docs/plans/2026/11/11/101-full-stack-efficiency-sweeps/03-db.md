## Tests
- `packages/db/src/slow-statement-log.test.ts` — a 600 ms statement logs once with its shape and correlation id and no bind value; a 100 ms one logs nothing; a burst of the same shape logs once per window.
- `packages/db/src/pool-gate.test.ts` — abort while queued: the waiter is gone, the next `leave()` admits the live waiter.
- `packages/db/src/table-storage.live.test.ts` — `reloptions` contains `fillfactor=70`, `relpersistence = 'u'`; drift is clean after apply and red after a manual `alter`.
- `packages/db/src/generate-index.live.test.ts` — shared with [`05`](05-entity.md).
- `packages/db/src/migration-lock-lint.test.ts` — a bare `set not null` is refused; the validated-check form and a marked line pass.
- `packages/db/src/prepared-pool.live.test.ts` (if D3 ships) — 10k calls: `total_plan_time` falls; `alter table` on an app table, then no `0A000`.
- Command: `bun test ./packages/db/src/<file>.test.ts`.
